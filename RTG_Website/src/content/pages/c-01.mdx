## Quality

- Welding coordinated by International Welding Engineers
- Welders to ISO 9606-1, DNV and LR
- Procedures approved with TÜV, DNV and LR
- NDT (UT, MT, PT) to ISO 9712 level 2
- Bureau Veritas Mode II workshop
- 100% traceability on main structural welds: weld number, welder punch, WPS, inspection record

## Required from you

- Fabrication drawings and weld map
- WPS (yours or ours for approval)
- Material specification and origin requirement
- NDT scope and acceptance criteria
- Class society and survey requirement
