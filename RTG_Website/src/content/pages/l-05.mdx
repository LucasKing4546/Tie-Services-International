## Assessment covers

- Drum and shaft condition
- Bearing wear
- Brake condition
- Hydraulic system and fluid condition
- Structural NDT
- Control system obsolescence

## You receive

A written report with reuse, rework or replace per component, upgrade options, costed recommendations and a spares list.
