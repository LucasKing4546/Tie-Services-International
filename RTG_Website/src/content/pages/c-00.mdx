## Approvals

Welding procedures approved with TÜV, DNV and Lloyd's Register. Welders qualified to ISO 9606-1, DNV and LR. Workshop recognised under the Bureau Veritas Mode II scheme.

## IP protection

- Mutual NDA before drawings are exchanged
- Segregated project files with restricted access
- No client design used in RTG products
- Documentation returned or destroyed at contract end
- Terms set out in the contract

[How we protect your design and IP](/contract-manufacturing/your-ip-protected/)

## EU manufacture

Fabricated in Satu Mare, Romania, inside the EU customs union, with melt-and-pour origin documentation supplied with the weld traceability pack.

New clients typically start with one sub-assembly at one tier against an agreed date.
