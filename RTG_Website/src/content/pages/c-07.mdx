Our factory is in Satu Mare, Romania, inside the EU customs union. Structure fabricated there is supplied with melt-and-pour origin documentation alongside our standard weld traceability pack.

<Gap>What changed on 1 July 2026, stated factually and without spin — the specific detail of the safeguard measures and who they affect.</Gap>

## What we cannot advise on

Origin and tariff treatment depend on the buyer's own supply chain and destination market, not just on where a structure was fabricated. We can supply the documentation. We cannot tell you what it means for your own position — that is a question for a customs adviser.

RTG's own tariff and origin position must be confirmed with a customs adviser before any definitive claim goes live here. An incorrect claim in a quotation is a real commercial risk, not a copy-editing one.
