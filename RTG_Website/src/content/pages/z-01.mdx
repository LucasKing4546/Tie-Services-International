Both UK GDPR and EU GDPR apply to us, because we design in the United Kingdom and manufacture in Romania.

## What we collect

**What you send us through a form.** Your name, company, work email address and the details of your enquiry: the equipment you are looking at, the vessel or project, your role, and anything you write in a message field. We ask for a work email address because datasheets are sent to it rather than linked.

**What a datasheet request adds.** Every datasheet we issue is watermarked with the recipient's name, company, the date and a unique serial number. We keep a record of which serial went to whom.

**What live chat collects.** The chat window on this site is provided by Chatra. If you use it, Chatra processes the messages you send, your IP address and basic browser information on our behalf. See the [cookie policy](/cookies/).

**What we do not collect.** This website runs no analytics and no advertising trackers. We do not buy contact lists and we do not build profiles of visitors.

## Why we hold it, and the lawful basis

| Purpose | Lawful basis |
|---|---|
| Answering your enquiry, quotation or datasheet request | Steps taken at your request before entering a contract |
| Keeping a record of documents issued to a named recipient | Our legitimate interest in protecting our drawings and technical publications |
| Following up a business enquiry you started | Our legitimate interest in responding to business contacts |
| Sending the bulletin, if you asked for it | Your consent, which you can withdraw at any time |

## Your rights

You can ask us to:

- give you a copy of the personal data we hold about you
- correct anything that is wrong
- delete it, where we have no continuing reason to keep it
- restrict or stop a use you object to, including any follow-up based on legitimate interest
- hand it to you in a portable format
- stop sending the bulletin, which you can also do from any issue

We answer within one month. There is no charge.

## How to contact us or complain

Write to us at the Satu Mare address above, or email [sales@romica.co.uk](mailto:sales@romica.co.uk) and mark the message "Data protection".
