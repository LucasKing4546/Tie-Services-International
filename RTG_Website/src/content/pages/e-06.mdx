## Class

Built to the same discipline as the rest of the range, whatever the duty
looks like on paper: design appraisal and drawing approval with the
nominated society before manufacture, survey during build with material
certification checked against the approved design and welding witnessed to
the approved procedures, and proof load testing on our own stands before
dispatch, with calibrated measuring equipment and certified test weights.
The machine ships with its test certificate, the test records behind it,
and material and weld traceability — not a certificate on its own, but the
evidence it rests on.

A mooring or utility winch rarely gets the attention a survey or seismic
machine does at the specification stage, and that is exactly the reasoning
that puts one on a foredeck without a rendering function it turns out to
need, or a brake margin sized to the calm-water figure rather than the
snatch load a slack tow line actually delivers. The load control valve
fitted to this family provides a genuine rendering function — it prevents
the peak loads that occur towing a wire in rough, slack conditions — and
the winch can be supplied configured underwound or overwound to suit the
lead-off.
