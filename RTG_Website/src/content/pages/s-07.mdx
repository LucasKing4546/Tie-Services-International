- Mill certificates, heat numbers and cast analysis recorded per part

- 100% traceability on main structural welds: weld number, welder marking punch, WPS and inspection record
- As-built configuration record against the frozen baseline, with every deviation and concession recorded
- Technical publications to the programme's specified standard
