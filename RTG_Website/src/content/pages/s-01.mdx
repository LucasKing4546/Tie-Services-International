## What we supply

- Survey and oceanographic winches, 300 to 4,000 m cable capacity, with or without level wind
- Closed-loop drives for stable payout at survey speeds down to 2 m/min
- Lebus shell grooving and controlled level wind on every survey winch
- Cable-out measurement for accurate layback
- Stern and side A-frames and mini survey LARS
- Containerised spreads for transfer between vessels
- Deck seating design, deck loading calculations, class appraisal and certification

## Testing

Every winch is static load, dynamic load and speed tested before dispatch, with line speed verified across all drum layers.

## Track record

Fugro has been a close strategic partner, with RTG handling systems across seven vessels over fourteen years. Survey clients also include Gardline and MMT.
