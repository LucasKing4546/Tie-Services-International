## What we supply

- Streamer reel winches with drums mounted on slew-ring bearings
- Multi-stage planetary drives with spring-applied, hydraulically released fail-safe brakes
- Source handling winches and deployment systems
- Modular frames that break down for road transport between mobilisations
- Matched units with consistent performance across the spread
