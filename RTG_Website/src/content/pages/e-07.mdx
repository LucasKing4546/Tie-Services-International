## Configurations

A rigid frame pivoting on deck-mounted supports, driven by hydraulic
cylinders through a synchronised local and remote proportional control
system, and built in modular segments so it can be disassembled for
standard road transport. Two real configurations are shown below: a 20 tonne
WLL unit with two cylinders per arm and a pair of auxiliary winches, and a
40 tonne WLL unit with one cylinder per arm, a head sheave and an auxiliary
winch. Both are proof load tested on our own stands before dispatch and
carry the same weld traceability as every structure that leaves this
factory.

## Sea state

An A-frame earns its place on the deck arrangement by working in conditions
everything else has stood down for. Both configurations are rated for
3.9 m significant wave height (Beaufort 5, and up to 6 on the 40 tonne unit)
— the figure that actually governs whether a recovery gets attempted, not
the calm-water capacity on the headline spec.

## Deck seating and loading

Deck seating design and deck loading calculations are supplied with the
equipment rather than commissioned separately — the question that most
often stalls a mobilisation is whether the deck will take the machine where
it needs to go, and on a chartered or newly-fitted hull nobody has the
answer to hand until it is worked out. Mount configuration and bolt pattern
are part of the same package, not an afterthought discovered at
installation.
