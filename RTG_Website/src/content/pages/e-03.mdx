- **Electric drive:** closed-loop control with encoder feedback, full torque at near-zero speed, regenerative payout
- **Hydraulic drive:** for vessels with limited electrical power or heavier duty
- **Level wind:** mechanical, electric or hydraulic actuation, specified for placement at low speed
- **Compact range:** chain-driven spooling from the winch gearmotor, sprocket and chain matched to cable diameter
- Speed tested across all drum layers before dispatch
