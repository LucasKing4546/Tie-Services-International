## What we supply

- ROV launch and recovery systems
- Umbilical and tow winches with Lebus grooved drums and PLC-controlled level wind
- Slip ring interface to suit the vehicle's power and data architecture; RTG procures, fits and function tests the specified unit
- Diver basket and man-riding systems with higher design factors, redundant braking, fail-safe behaviour on loss of power and a defined secondary recovery path
- Design appraisal, survey during build and proof-load certification with every machine

Personnel systems are built to the framework required by the operator and their class society, stated on each project.

## Clients

DeepOcean, Ocean Infinity and Fugro.
