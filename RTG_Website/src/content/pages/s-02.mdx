## What we supply

- Corer handling systems: piston, gravity, box and vibrocorer
- CPT deployment and recovery systems
- Tension control winches with continuous line-in, line-out and tension readout on the HMI
- Drum flanges drilled for stoppering off with chain and shackles
- A-frames and LARS sized to the tool and the deck
- Containerised and skid-mounted spreads for chartered vessels

## Sizing at depth

Line pull falls and line speed rises as the drum fills. On one RTG multifunctional winch, WLL is 2,545 kg on the first layer and 1,500 kg on the top layer; line speed is 50 m/min and 90 m/min respectively. Geotechnical winches are sized to the first-layer figure at working depth.
