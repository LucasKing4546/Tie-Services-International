## The standard we target

This website is built to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2 at level AA.

In practice that means:

- every page has one main heading and a heading structure you can navigate by
- a "Skip to content" link is the first thing a keyboard reaches
- links, buttons, form fields and carousels can be operated from the keyboard, and show a visible focus ring when they have focus
- pages reflow to a single column on a narrow screen without scrolling sideways
- motion is reduced or removed when your device asks for reduced motion
- form fields have visible labels, and required fields are marked in text as well as colour

## Known issues

We would rather list these than have you find them.

- **Three-dimensional models.** The models on the homepage are drawn on a canvas. Each has a text label saying what it shows, but the model itself cannot be explored with a screen reader. What the models illustrate is also written out as text on the page.
- **Scroll-driven sections.** On a wide screen some sections hold in place while their content changes as you scroll. With reduced motion switched on, or on a narrow screen, they become ordinary scrolling content.
- **Photographs.** Photography is still being added. Each photograph has a text description, and some of those descriptions are short and will be improved as the final images are confirmed.
- **Live chat.** The chat window is supplied by a third party and we do not control its accessibility. Every enquiry it handles can also be made through the [contact page](/contact/).
- **Cookie notice.** The cookie notice and its settings panel are supplied by a third party, OneTrust, and we do not control their accessibility. Both can be operated from the keyboard. If you cannot use them, tell us and we will record your choice for you.
- **Issued documents.** Datasheets and drawings are issued as watermarked images of each page, which a screen reader cannot read. Tell us and we will send the same figures in an accessible format.

This website has been tested by its developers with a keyboard and with automated checks. It has not yet had an independent accessibility audit.

## Telling us about a problem

If something on this website does not work for you, or you need information from it in another format, email [sales@romica.co.uk](mailto:sales@romica.co.uk) with "Accessibility" in the subject line, or use the [contact page](/contact/). Tell us the page and what you were trying to do.
