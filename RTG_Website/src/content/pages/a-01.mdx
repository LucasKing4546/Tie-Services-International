## The model, and why it was built that way

Steelwork was originally subcontracted to class-certified fabricators, while assembly, electro-hydraulic and control integration, and testing stayed in-house at Satu Mare. Machine tools were bought back year after year until fabrication came in-house too — a twenty-year strategy told in three sentences. Design authority stays in the UK; manufacturing depth is in Romania. There is no part of the process RTG cannot see.

It also answers the question a client of an 80-person company is entitled to ask: will you still be here in ten years?

## What we believe
