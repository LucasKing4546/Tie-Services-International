## The short version

When you first visit, a notice asks whether you accept cookies. Until you answer, this website loads no analytics and no chat, and sets nothing that is not strictly necessary.

You can change your answer at any time: <a href="/cookies/" data-cookie-settings>open cookie settings</a>. The same link is at the foot of every page.

## Categories

The settings panel groups what this site can store into categories. Strictly necessary items are always on. Everything else is off until you switch it on.

**Strictly necessary.** The record of your own cookie choice, so that we do not ask again on every page. It is kept by OneTrust, the service that provides the notice and the settings panel. Fonts, styles and images are served from this site, not from a third party.

**Performance.** Google Analytics. It tells us how many people visit, which pages they read and roughly where they come from. It collects statistical data such as your IP address, operating system and browser. We use it to see trends, not to identify you.

**Functional: live chat.** The chat window is provided by Chatra. It keeps a small identifier in your browser so that a conversation continues as you move between pages and when you come back. At present the settings panel has no separate switch for the chat, so it loads only if you choose "Accept All Cookies".

**Targeting.** This website sets none. It carries no advertising and no embedded video.

## Changing your mind

<a href="/cookies/" data-cookie-settings>Open cookie settings</a> to switch any category on or off. A change takes effect at once for anything not yet loaded, and from the next page for anything already running.

Two other ways to refuse:

- **Google Analytics only.** Google provides a browser add-on that stops Analytics on every website you visit: [Google Analytics opt-out add-on](https://tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout).
- **Everything.** Every browser lets you block or clear what a site has stored. This website works the same without any of it. Only the chat window is affected.
