## The short version

This website does not use analytics cookies or advertising cookies. The previous version of the site used Google Analytics. This one does not.

The only third party that stores anything on your device is the live chat window.

## Categories

**Strictly necessary.** Nothing is set by the pages themselves. Fonts, styles and images are served from this site, not from a third party.

**Functional: live chat.** The chat window is provided by Chatra. It stores a small identifier in your browser so that a conversation continues as you move between pages and when you come back.

**Analytics.** None.

**Marketing.** None.

## Changing your mind

<Gap>RTG to supply: a working consent control. The chat window currently loads without asking. Before launch it must be held back until the visitor agrees, and this section must link to the control that reopens that choice.</Gap>

Until then you can refuse or remove these items yourself. Every browser lets you block third-party storage or clear what a site has stored, and the rest of this website works the same without it. Only the chat window is affected.
