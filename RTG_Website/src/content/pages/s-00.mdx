Every one of these markets is the same engineering problem wearing different
clothes.

RTG designs, manufactures and supplies lifting and handling systems for vessels that
work over the side. Alongside a standard range of proven products, equipment is
designed and built to a specific vessel, a specific tool and a specific deck.

We supply complete after-deck equipment packages rather than individual machines,
scoped to what the project actually needs. Most of this work is cost-driven, and the
job is to find the arrangement that meets the requirement rather than the one that
sells the most steel.

The sectors below differ less in the engineering than they do in what failure costs:
a lost cast, a lost tool, a lost day of a spread already in the water, or a vessel
that cannot be handed over. Each page starts from that.
