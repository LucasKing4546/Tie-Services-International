## Certification

- Design appraisal and drawing approval with the nominated society before manufacture
- Survey during build; welding witnessed to approved procedures; agreed hold points
- Static proof load and functional load testing in-house to <Fig of="loadTestTe" />, with calibrated equipment and certified test weights
- Test certificate, test records, material and weld traceability and manuals supplied with every crane
- Periodic thorough examination and re-certification of cranes in service, including other manufacturers' equipment

When supplied with RTG winches and A-frames, the crane shares one deck seating design, one set of loading calculations, one class submission and one certification dossier.
