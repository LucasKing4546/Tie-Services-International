Anyone can claim to be certified. This is what a yard's procurement and quality team can actually check before we go on an approved vendor list.

## Why traceability is the item that matters

A yard carries the class exposure for work it did not do itself — that is the real reason a vendor approval process exists, and it is why the traceability line above outweighs most of the rest of the table.

Every main structural weld we make is recorded against a weld number, the marking punch of the welder who made it, the procedure specification it was made to, and the inspection record. That record travels with the part, not just with our files.
