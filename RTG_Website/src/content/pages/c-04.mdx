## What we do

Your PLC and HMI software loaded and configured, function and interlock proving, then the machine tested on our own stands: static structural proof load, dynamic functional load, static brake holding, render and speed tests, up to <Fig of="loadTestTe" /> with calibrated equipment and certified test weights. Class witness where required. Full certification dossier. Then commissioning on site or on the vessel, with operator familiarisation.

## What you no longer do

Receive a machine and find out what is wrong with it on the quay. A factory acceptance test moves that discovery to a place where it is cheap to fix.

<Note heading="What we need from you">
PLC and HMI software, and the control philosophy. Test procedure and acceptance criteria. Class society witness requirement. Documentation format for the dossier. Commissioning location, dates and vessel access arrangements.
</Note>

## How it is proven

A signed test record against your acceptance criteria, load test certificates, the class surveyor's endorsement where applicable, and a complete certification and documentation dossier issued with the machine.

<Note tone="warn" heading="Be straight with us about scope">
Tier 4 commits our controls and commissioning engineers to your programme, sometimes offshore. We would rather agree Tier 3 and a defined witness scope than promise a commissioning date we cannot hold. Ask us what our current commissioning capacity looks like before you plan around it.
</Note>
