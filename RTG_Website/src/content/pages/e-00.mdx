Everything here is engineered to a duty. None of it is picked off a shelf.

That is not a boast, it is a description of how the work arrives. Nobody has ever
come to us wanting a winch. They come wanting to put a corer on the seabed at
<Fig of="cptDepthM" />, or recover an ROV in a sea state that was not in the plan,
or move a survey spread onto a hull they have chartered for the season. The
machine is what falls out of that conversation.

## Why the figures on these pages are representative

Each equipment page publishes the headline performance figures openly — working
load limits at first and top layer, line speeds, brake margins, supply
requirements and an overall envelope. Enough to know whether we are in the right
area, whether it will fit your deck, and whether it is worth a conversation.

They are figures for a configuration, not a catalogue entry. The unit we build
for you will differ, sometimes considerably.

The full datasheet — the dimensioned general arrangement, mounting and seating
detail, interface envelope and options — is issued on request rather than
published. Every copy is watermarked to the recipient and confidential to them.
That lets us hand real engineering detail to the people who need it, instead of
publishing it for everyone who does not. If you need it, ask and we will send
it, usually the same day.

## Three ways in

- **By sector** — if you know the market you work in. Survey, geotechnical, wind
  and cables, seismic, research, subsea, defence. [Sectors →](/sectors/)
- **By application** — if you know the job. Piston coring, CPT deployment, CTD
  casts, ROV launch, deep tow, cable lay. Each equipment page below lists what
  it is used for.
- **By duty** — if you know the numbers and want a shortlist. Tell us the tool,
  the depth, the load and the vessel, and an engineer will come back with
  configurations and what is still missing.
  [Request a configuration →](/contact/request-a-quotation/)

## What we need to size anything

Three things get us most of the way: what you are deploying, how deep, and what
deck it has to work from. Everything else is refinement. If you have a deck
arrangement, send it — it saves a round trip.
