## Applications

A seismic deck is a dozen machines that have to behave like one — winches
deployed together, recovered together, holding tension together, handling
source arrays and digital streamer cable that stays in the water for weeks
at a time. One unit that runs slightly differently from the one beside it
is not a maintenance issue; it is a geometry issue, and geometry is what a
seismic vessel is being paid to acquire.

This family is built around that: a reel-style drum mounted directly on a
slew-ring bearing rather than a conventional shaft-mounted design, driven
through a toothed pinion off a multi-stage planetary gearbox with a
hydraulically actuated, spring-applied fail-safe brake. The frame and drum
are manufactured in modular segments so the machine can be disassembled for
standard road transport between mobilisations.
