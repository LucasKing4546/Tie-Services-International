- Line tension and squeeze measured independently: four load pins with calibrated weighing modules per track
- Squeeze adjustable from 0.5 to 25 Te to match the cable's permitted crush load
- V-grooved, rubber-bonded, bolt-on pads matched to the product
- Fail-safe braked hydraulic motors: spring applied, pressure released; product held on loss of power
- Static parking brake minimum 1.8 × SWL
- Remote console over a single 20 m Ethernet cable; rotary controllers, illuminated push buttons, 9-inch HMI with full alarm and monitoring
- Brake holding proven on our stands before dispatch

## To specify

We need the cable outside diameter and construction, minimum bend radius (static and dynamic), permitted crush load, required line tension at lay depth, the vessel and the date.
