## Site investigation

- Corer, CPT and vibrocorer handling systems with constant tension control
- Short cycle times and consistent deployment across multi-position campaigns
- Containerised and skid-mounted spreads for chartered vessels
- Deck seating design and deck loading calculations supplied with the equipment
- Same tension control technology as RTG's <Fig of="cptDepthM" /> CPT record

## Cable handling

- Two-track electro-hydraulic tensioners, 5 to 20 Te, self-contained on one skid
- Integrated hydraulic power unit; requires an electrical supply only
- Line tension and squeeze measured independently by load pins on each track
- Squeeze adjustable from 0.5 to 25 Te to suit the cable's permitted crush load
- Fail-safe braked hydraulic motors; static parking brake minimum 1.8 × SWL
- Remote console over a single 20 m Ethernet cable, 9-inch HMI with full alarm and monitoring
- Road transportable, craned on and commissioned in a shift

## Vessels of opportunity

Each spread arrives complete with power unit, controls and guarding, certified lifting lugs, fork pockets, a stated footprint and twist-lock option. Lead-off geometry and interfaces are specified for transfer between hulls.
