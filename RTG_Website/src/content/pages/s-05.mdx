## What we supply

- **Profiling and CTD winches:** closed-loop electric drives holding a steady 2 m/min at depth; turndown better than 45:1; regenerative payout
- **Coring and seabed sampling:** piston, gravity, box and vibrocorer handling with tension control
- **Towed bodies and deep tow:** Lebus grooved drums, controlled level wind, accurate wire-out measurement
- **Launch and recovery:** stern and side A-frames and mini survey LARS, with deck seating and loading calculations
- **Portable and containerised spreads** for chartered and shared vessels: certified lifting lugs, fork pockets, stated footprint, single power supply

## Design choices

- **Drive:** electric for precise slow-speed control and clean installation; hydraulic where vessel power is limited or the duty is heavy
- **Level wind:** mechanical, electric or hydraulic actuation, specified for accurate placement at low speed
- **Interfaces:** sensors, terminations, slip rings and data systems are supplied by the institution; RTG designs the winch to accept them

## Testing

Every winch is speed tested across all drum layers before dispatch; results are recorded in the test document.
