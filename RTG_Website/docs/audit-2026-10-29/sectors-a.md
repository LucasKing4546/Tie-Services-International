# Audit: sectors-a (S-00 /sectors/, S-01 /sectors/ocean-survey-hydrography/, S-02 /sectors/marine-geotechnical/)

Review only, no repo files touched. Screenshots and scripts are in `scratchpad/audit/sectors-a/` (full-page `*-desktop.png` / `*-mobile.png`, viewport `s0X-{d,m}-hero.png`, `s00-focus-card.png`, `report.json`). The scripts are `scratchpad/pw/sa-*.mjs`.

## Integrity summary
- All three pages have exactly one h1, and it matches the workbook h1 word for word. Heading order is h1 > h2 > h3, with no skipped levels.
- HTTP 200 on all three. No console errors apart from the expected blocked OneTrust request. No horizontal overflow at 1440 or 390. No `<img>` is missing its alt.
- Reduced motion: no `.rl`, `.fu` or `.stag` element stays hidden under `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce`.
- There are no em or en dashes and no exclamation marks. None of the §8 banned words appear. Spelling is British.
- Gaps: S-02 carries one `<Gap>` ("RTG to supply: operator, vessel and year, if cleared.", `s-02.mdx:31`), so it is correctly `noindex, follow`. S-00 and S-01 carry no gap markers and are indexable.
- §6: no drum core, length or flange diameter and no GA drawings. "Drum flanges drilled for stoppering off" is a feature, not a dimension. "Lebus" grooving (`s-01.mdx:29`) is a LeBus International trade name (see Low).

## Critical
None.

## High
1. **S-01: the second photograph is mis-captioned and has the hallmarks of a third-party image.** `IMG-S01-C` → `src/assets/sectors/s01-deck-spread.jpg` (registered at `src/data/media.ts:52,102`) is a CGI render: a vessel, a multibeam swath cone and coloured bathymetry. Its caption and alt say "Containerised survey spread rigged on deck, ready for mobilisation." (`s-01.mdx:22`). The file is 1200x840 with a `gd-jpeg v1.0` (PHP GD) comment, which is typical of an image downloaded from the web, not an RTG camera original. media.ts's own provenance note vouches only for `s01-equipment-*` as camera originals. Evidence: `sectors_ocean-survey-hydrography-desktop.png` (photo band, right tile). **Fix:** unregister `IMG-S01-C` until ownership is confirmed. If it stays, rewrite the alt and caption to say what the image actually shows.
2. **S-01 and S-02 do not lead with the buyer's problem (§8), and workbook blocks are missing.**
   - S-01 workbook blocks (`pages.ts:84`): "The problem in the buyer's words" is absent. The page opens with a capability list.
   - S-02 (`pages.ts:98`): "The captive-tool problem" and "tension control explained" have no section of their own. The problem appears only as half a clause in the lede. "Sizing at depth" covers layer pull, not tension control.
   - Word counts are about 306 against a target of 700 (S-01) and about 316 against 900 (S-02). The template doc comment (`Sector.astro:8-11`) calls the brevity deliberate, but the problem block is a DoD item.
   - **Fix:** open each body with a short h2 problem paragraph. For S-01 that is stable payout at 2 m/min and accurate layback. For S-02 it is the tool fixed in the seabed while the vessel heaves, and what constant tension does about it. Take the wording from the copy document, or mark it with a `<Gap>`.
3. **Hero breadcrumbs over photographs fail contrast.** The crumbs are 11px mono text at `rgba(255,255,255,.58)` (`blocks.css:80`) and sit at the top of the hero, where the scrim is weakest (`blocks.css:73-76`). Measured backgrounds give an effective contrast of about 2.2:1 on S-02 desktop and about 2.7:1 on S-01 desktop. AA needs 4.5:1. **Fix:** raise inactive crumbs to `.8` alpha on `.phero:has(.phero-media-shot)`, or add a top-down scrim stop (around 0.45 for the first 30%).

## Medium
4. **S-00: the hub's "seven sector tiles with photography" have no photography** (`pages.ts:70`). The seven cards are text only and 430px tall, and the copy fills about half of each, so every card has a large empty lower half (`s00-focus-card.png`). The hub hero also has no image, so the right half of the first screen is empty water (`sectors-desktop.png`). **Fix:** give `ChildCards` a card image from each child's hero ref, since S-01 and S-02 already have one. Until then, shorten the cards to fit their content.
5. **S-00: the CTA sentence is separated from its buttons.** "Send us the vessel, the tool and the working depth, and an engineer will propose a configuration." (`s-00.mdx:14`) renders as a display-size `lead-para` section between the hero and the rail (`Hub.astro:100-105`). The buttons it introduces come about 1,000px later with no text. **Fix:** move the sentence into frontmatter `cta.heading` or `cta.body` so it sits with the ending buttons, and drop the body section.
6. **All three pages end with a bare duplicate button pair.** No `cta` is set in frontmatter, so `endingCta()` (`lib/blocks.ts:92-93`) repeats the hero buttons. `CtaBand` then renders them with no heading (`CtaBand.astro:16-21`) in a hairline-topped section, so two orphan pills float above the proof bar (bottom of every desktop and mobile screenshot). **Fix:** give each page a `cta.heading` (S-02's workbook CTA: "Talk to a geotechnical engineer"), or have `PageEnding` skip the band when it would only duplicate the hero.
7. **S-01: the "Technical note" link promises an article that does not exist.** "Technical note: Low-speed control, level wind reversal and layer change in survey winches" (`s-01.mdx:43`) links to the generic `/resources/technical-notes/` hub, and the hub does not list that note. **Fix:** cut the specific title (for example "More in Technical notes") until the note exists, or mark it with a `<Gap>`.
8. **S-02: internal ID in public copy.** "(case study CS-02)" (`s-02.mdx:39`, and the same in `l-00.mdx:28`) is a workbook code, not something a buyer recognises. **Fix:** link descriptive text instead, for example "read the refurbishment case study".
9. **Section rhythm leaves large empty bands, against §7 "compact".**
   - S-01: the lists section's bottom padding of 86px plus the photo band's top padding of 86px, under a short right column, leaves about 230px of empty water before the photos (`sectors_ocean-survey-hydrography-desktop.png`, y≈1250-1330 in the original).
   - S-02: the same between the lists and "Related equipment" (`sectors_marine-geotechnical-desktop.png`).
   - **Fix:** use `size="sm"` on the Sector lists `Section` (`Sector.astro:48`), or collapse the padding of adjacent `.sec` + `.sec`.
10. **S-01 and S-02: the two list columns' tops do not align.** `.prose.lists` uses CSS columns (`blocks.css:698-701`). Only the first h2 loses its top margin, so the h2 that starts column 2 keeps its hairline and padding and sits 10-12px below "What we supply", which has no rule. **Fix:** give the first h2 of each column the same rule (for example `border-top` on every h2, including the first), or switch to a two-cell grid.
11. **OG images 404 for all three pages.** `og:image` points at `/assets/og/s-0X.jpg` (`lib/seo.ts:8`, `Base.astro:77`), but no such files exist in `public/` or `dist/`. `og:image:alt` describes an image that isn't there ("Composite of four sector images in a grid"). This is a site-wide issue. **Fix:** generate the cards, or omit `og:image` until they exist.
12. **Alt text on the S-01 and S-02 heroes is the shoot brief, not a description.** S-02's alt "…lowering a geotechnical frame over the stern in a rising sea" describes a scene the misty deck photo does not clearly show (`s-02.mdx:8`, `PageHero.astro:42`). §5.6 is a known gap, but for real photos the brief is now in place of alt text. **Fix:** write true alt text for the four registered sector photos.

## Low
13. **S-01: the busy hero photograph competes with the h1.** A saturated blue winch and cage sit directly behind "Survey and hydrographic". The h1 passes 3:1 on 98% of samples (p98 3.19:1), but only just (`s01-d-hero.png`). **Fix:** use `object-position` to push the machine right, or use a stronger left scrim stop.
14. **S-02 mobile hero image is soft.** It uses `sizes="100vw"` with `object-fit:cover` in a 390x578 box (`PageHero.astro:44`), so a 900w landscape file is cover-scaled to about 1,027px wide. At DPR 3 the upscale is about 2x. **Fix:** on ≤900px use `sizes` of about `(max-width:900px) 260vw, 100vw` for landscape heroes.
15. **The S-01 mobile breadcrumb wraps,** leaving a trailing "/" at the end of line 1 and the current-page pill on line 2 (`s01-m-hero.png`). **Fix:** truncate the current crumb or allow `flex-wrap` with the separator attached to the next item.
16. **Breadcrumb labels are inconsistent.** The parent reads "Sectors hub", not the nav's "Sectors". The current crumbs use Title Case with "&" ("Ocean Survey & Hydrography"), while the cards and h1 use "Ocean survey and hydrography". The source is `page.name` in `pages.ts`. **Fix:** use a short sentence-case crumb label.
17. **S-00: the rail heading "Everything in sectors" is generic** (`Hub.astro:61`). **Fix:** use something like "Choose your sector" (the workbook CTA is "Enter your sector").
18. **S-00: card link text capitalises mid-sentence** ("Read about Ocean survey and hydrography →"), and each whole-card link has a very long accessible name (h3, paragraph and arrow text). **Fix:** lower-case the interpolated name, and use an `aria-label` equal to the h3.
19. **S-00: keyboard focus on the 4th and 7th cards leaves them partly off-screen** (x=1268 at a 1440 viewport). The rail does not scroll a focused card fully into view (`s00-focus-card.png`). This passes 2.4.11 because the card is only partly obscured, but it looks wrong. **Fix:** set `scroll-padding-inline` on `.strack-rail`, or `scrollIntoView({inline:'nearest'})` on focusin.
20. **Dead eyebrow markup.** `ScrollTrack` and `ChildCards` still render `<p class="mono on-navy fu">Sectors</p>`, but `ocean.css:56` hides it with `display:none`. **Fix:** stop passing `eyebrow` in `Hub.astro:120`, or drop the hide rule.
21. **S-01 photo band treatment is off-system.** Captions are frosted, `backdrop-filter` plates overlaid on the photos (`parts-sections.css:20-24`). They cover about a quarter of the 245px-tall mobile tile, and a hover zoom (`parts-sections.css:31-35`) goes against §7's "no scattered hover effects". The alt text also repeats the figcaption word for word, so screen readers read it twice. `figure` is nested in `figure` (`MediaBand.astro:64-66`). **Fix:** put the caption under the image, use `alt=""` or a distinct alt, and drop the hover scale.
22. **"Lebus shell grooving"** (`s-01.mdx:29`) names a LeBus International trade name. §6 bans component makes. **Fix:** confirm it is acceptable as a generic term, or write "parallel (counter-balanced) grooving".
23. **S-02: the gap chip wraps badly.** "RTG to supply: operator, vessel and year, if / cleared." breaks onto a second line in mono (`sectors_marine-geotechnical-desktop.png`). The gap is intentional. Only its line break is cosmetic.
24. **Stale comment.** `media.ts:24-30` says no page other than the homepage names Fugro, but S-01 and S-02 now name Fugro under the §5.7 decision of 2026-10-08. **Fix:** update the comment.
