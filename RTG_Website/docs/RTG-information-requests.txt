A. ERRORS / INCONSISTENCIES IN THE DATASHEETS AND FILES

A1. DS57 (cable/pipe tensioner) - titled "15 Te" but its maximum tension still reads "0 - 10 Te", identical to the 10 Te sheet (same speed, squeeze range and weight). What is the real maximum tension? The website currently shows this value as "to be confirmed".
A2. DS80 (30 Te mooring winch without level wind) - overall height is printed as "19460 mm" (looks like a typo for about 1946). Its dry weight is not stated at all. Please give both.
A3. DS83 (0.24 Te winch) - the file name says 700 m; the table says 750 m at 8.18 mm cable (1,250 m at 6 mm, 510 m at 10 mm, 330 m at 12 mm). The website uses the table. Please confirm.
A4. DS61 (5 Te gravity corer chute) - the overall envelope is ambiguous (two sets of dimensions; unclear which is stowed and which is deployed). Please confirm both, labelled.
A5. DS33 (40 Te A-frame) and DS96 (20 Te A-frame) were filed in the "Cranes & Lifting" folder. We treated them as launch-and-recovery A-frames (E-07). Please confirm that is right.
A6. Mooring photos IMG_0287 and 3.jpg have "SWL 50TE" stencilled on the frame, but the datasheet (DS74) is 30 Te WLL. Is the 50 Te a lifting/base rating, or is this a different unit?
A7. DS38 datasheet: the drawing title block says capacity "1,300 m" while the table and title say 2,300 m. Which is correct?
A8. The datasheets say "Design: Lloyds" without saying what that means. Please state exactly which class rules each unit is designed to.
